# Engineering retrospective — [feature] — [date]

## 1. Outcome
Exact delivered state, strongest practices, principal friction, validation limits and selected improvements.

## 2. Scope and actual participants
Period/cutoff; actual roles; requested versus confirmed settings; replacement/gap and first-hand evidence limits. Never invent attendance.

## 3. Factual timeline
Approved DI/revisions; candidates; executed checks; gate dispositions; actual user steering; delivery/publication boundary. Separate identity from execution.

## 4. Independent reflections
Collect before sharing peers' responses. Attribute paraphrases, evidence, impact, surprises and one/two improvement ideas.

## 5. Real discussion and changed views
Questions/responses, clarified facts, disagreement, evidence and conclusions. Preserve initial reflections separately.

## 6. Prioritization
Actual top ideas and reasons from participants; selection rationale and dissent. No invented votes.

## 7. Selected experiments — one to three
| Problem / change | Accountable role | Authorized trigger | Success criterion | Effectiveness check | Dependencies / status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [bounded experiment] | [agreed role] | [next workflow/actual agreed date] | [observable] | [future case check] | [proposed/accepted/done] |

Recommendations do not automatically install policy, schedule automations or assign humans. Do not promise gains unsupported by measurements.

## 8. Backlog and limits
Deferred ideas, unrun platforms/checks, open product questions and authority-dependent next steps.

## 9. Complete-draft accuracy and feedback
Send all sections to actual participants. Incorporate corrections, preserve dissent, mark missing responses as gaps. Distinguish source, fixture, oracle, selection, environment and dashboard problems.

## 10. Authorized publication and readback
Destination/audience; duplicate check; actual title/parent/content/tables/links; complete local draft and verified URL. If blocked, report the exact blocker. Create response alone is not verification.
